## Verdict and decomposition

**Registered verdict: mixed/unresolved, 18/18 deep (run, layer) cells** —
and uniformly so in the 27 shallow context rows. The value is in the
decomposition, because both hypotheses fail their own signatures
decisively rather than ambiguously:

- **Sharpening fails on identity and morphing.** S1 (structured-unit
  Jaccard, early vs late) median 0.17 across all 45 rows (deep only:
  0.06); only 2/45 exceed the 0.5 persistence bar. Among units that do
  persist, the overtone slope S2a is immaterial or negative everywhere —
  no unit morphs sinusoid -> square.
- **Replacement fails on waveform at birth.** S2b (overtone at structure
  onset for late-arriving units): median 0.012, maximum 0.032 across all
  45 rows — an order of magnitude below the square-wave reference (0.111)
  and below the 0.06 threshold in 0/45 rows. Newly structured units are
  born sinusoidal, in every family, at every depth. The frequent S3
  bimodality (delta-BIC up to ~130) has sub-cell upper modes (<= 0.8
  purity), i.e. two sinusoid saturation populations, not a cell mode.

**Post hoc reading (labeled as such; not pre-registered):** the data
describe a third mechanism — **churn of ever-sinusoidal units with
population-composition drift**. Structured units continuously enter and
leave the structured set; newcomers always arrive wave-like; no unit
transforms and no unit is born quantized. The aggregate depth-rise of
concentration and purity (finding 13 of `ORDER_PARAMETER_RESULTS.md`) is
therefore a property of the population distribution shifting toward
more-saturated sinusoids, not of any unit-level transition. On this task
family, at these depths, "understanding" never localizes into stable,
quantized, unit-level logic — it remains a drifting, distributed wave
code even while the population statistics sharpen.

**Implication for the amendment-6 Part B (deep-5) arm, unchanged and
still registered:** the arm now tests whether five nonlinearities (a)
finally produce born-quantized or morphing units (either world's
signature appearing where 3 layers show neither), or (b) extend the
churn picture, strengthening the case that the LIC bifurcation does not
exist on modular arithmetic at trainable depths.
