## 3. Competitive Attractor Dynamics

Grokking time $\tau$ is composed of a deterministic computation time ($t_R$) and a stochastic release delay ($\Delta \tau$):
$$ \tau = t_R + \Delta \tau $$

### **The "Smoking Gun": The CV Peak**
First-principles evidence for metastability is found in the **Coefficient of Variation ($CV = \sigma / \mu$)**. While $t_R$ remains deterministic ($CV \approx 0.1$), stochasticity in $\Delta \tau$ peaks in the transition zone ($p=47, CV = 0.28$). This is the signature of a **Bifurcation Point** where the memorization well becomes unstable.

## 4. Substrate Friction: The Spectral Ratio Hypothesis

The **Substrate Friction Coefficient ($\eta$)**, which determines the transition velocity from $R$ to $D$, is the macroscopic realization of the **Hessian Spectral Topology**.

Optimization is confined to an **Execution Manifold** ($H_{\parallel}$) of fast eigenvalues. Deployment is trapped in the **Transverse Manifold** ($H_{\perp}$) of slow eigenvalues.

$$ \rho(t) = \frac{\langle \lambda_{\text{fast}} \rangle}{\langle \lambda_{\text{slow}} \rangle} $$

Grokking occurs when the **release capacity** ($\lambda_{\text{fast}}$) overcomes the **confinement friction** ($\lambda_{\text{slow}}$).
